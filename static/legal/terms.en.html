4. Roles and rights

Rights in the Service follow the principle of least privilege: each role receives only the capabilities its tasks require.

  • Guest — browses the catalog and reads free books.
  • Reader — additionally keeps shelves and bookmarks, rates books, writes, edits and deletes own comments, may unlock a paid new release and apply for student status.
  • Student — a reader who has verified their status with a student ID; reads paid new releases for free.
  • Author — adds books, edits and deletes only their own books, may make a book temporarily paid.
  • Editor — manages the book catalog and moderates comments on books.
  • Moderator — moderates comments and users: bans and unbans with a stated reason and term, reviews unban and student-status requests, views the activity log. Does not manage the book catalog.
  • Lead developer — the Service administrator: assigns roles, deletes accounts and holds all rights of the other roles.
